# 0.8.0 (retroactive)

0.8.0 is a label applied after the fact, on 2026-09-12, to mark the end of the July build-out. It points at a commit from 2026-07-29. No build ever reported this number: the manifests carried other versions at the time, and the tag changed no code. These notes summarise the main features in the history up to that point, from the project's start on 2026-03-30. They do not list every change.

## For members

- Sessions: RSVP ahead of time (going or can't make it) with a calendar link, a personal calendar subscription feed (ICS), check-in by scanning a QR code at the door, and a visible reliability record that execs can adjust.
- Legal: accept the liability waiver and code of conduct, with terms of use and a privacy and data retention policy; the waiver renews every year, and a tournament can carry its own event waiver.
- Account deletion with a 30-day window in which you can restore the account yourself.
- Notifications in the app and by push, with per-category preferences, for announcements and tournament registration.
- Avatars everywhere (feed, leaderboard, stats, challenges), a photo you can position and zoom, and a bio on your public profile.
- A profile QR code that opens a pre-filled challenge.
- Ratings move slightly more for a sweep than for a three-game win.

## For execs and admins

- The console requires a passkey to sign in, and can be installed as an app.
- Attendance marking with no-shows and check-in windows, recurring session series with excluded dates, and session reminders sent by hand.
- Force a waiver re-signature, and edit the waiver and code of conduct text.
- Pause and resume a tournament with a reason.
- Audit log filters, a platform settings page, and Suspended and Inactive roster tabs.
- Errors from pages and server actions are reported to Sentry.
