# Release 1.0.0

1.0.0 is the first official release of the club app: sessions, the ladder, challenges, tournaments and your stats in one place, with an exec console beside it. It reached production on 2026-09-13.

The number went backwards on purpose. The manifests had drifted to 1.5.1 through dependency bumps without any version ever being agreed, so 1.0.0 is the first number that means a release. The work before it is labelled retroactively as [0.8.0](0.8.0.md) (to the end of July) and [0.9.0](0.9.0.md) (to the end of August). The notes below cover what changed between 0.9.0 and 1.0.0.

## For members

- Self-serve sign-ups can be approved without waiting for an exec, while the club has that switched on.
- In Discord, @Internal, @Alumni and @External are now the member's own pick; the nightly role sweep no longer takes them back.
- Website announcements can carry formatting (bold, italics and the like), and the app renders it.
- Discord match results read as a matchup ("Winner vs Loser") with the score in its own field, one line per game.
- The Discord profile card (`/profile`) is rebuilt as a grid of panels, shows all four ranks (or one with `type:`), and draws the bio on the card.
- A self-updating session board in Discord, and paged lists so `/sessions`, `/sessionpost` and `/tournaments` reach every row.
- Guide messages in Discord carry buttons to connect your club account, report a bug or send feedback.
- The Execs link in the top bar is a real link again.

## For execs and admins

- Pick several members, or several session nights, and act on all of them at once: mass edit for the roster, sessions and both fee desks.
- Set console access from the roster, and edit a member's full permissions on their own page.
- The roster has an All tab and opens on it.
- Hide an officer from the public Execs page without changing their access.
- Attach a receipt photo to a club expense.
- Send a message to Discord from the console: a channel picker, role chips (every role in the server, not only the nine the app manages), a ping line above an embed, member buttons, a preview of what Discord will show, and editing of what was already posted.
- `announcements.discord.write` belongs to VP External by default.
- New Discord commands for execs: `/say` (post as the club), `/announce` (file a website announcement from Discord), `/sessionpost` (publish the schedule into a channel), `/config` (set the relay channels), `/guidepost` and `/discord` (the club invite as a link and a QR code).
- The ratings screen no longer labels a field "Starting rating" that no new member's rating was ever taken from.
- The dashboard's "in play" tournaments are scoped to the season.

## Under the hood

### Security

- Challenge and result rules are enforced in the database rather than trusted to the browser: a challenge's stakes cannot be changed after it is made, a member cannot enter somebody else's challenge, only the opposing side can confirm a doubles result, and an account suspended after a challenge was proposed can no longer accept it.
- A settings save can no longer delete a key it was not sent.
- A bulk edit cannot let a pending sign-up in by the back door.

### Other

- Signed-in pages make fewer sign-in lookups: public paths no longer pay for one, and the viewer's row is fetched once per request.
- Every part of the repository has a README.

### Measured speed

- **Public pages** (the leaderboard, the legal pages and the like) no longer ask the auth server who you are. Counted per request on the staging copy of the site: 2 checks before, 1 after on every public page measured.
- **Your own member record** was read with several differently shaped queries on one page (the layout and each page asked for different columns), so none of them could be shared. It is now read once per page with one shape. The exec page also lost its second auth check.

## Before you deploy / owner steps

1. **Apply the migrations in numeric order**, 00220 to 00231. 00224 must follow 00223. 00223, 00229, 00230 and 00231 have no `BEGIN`/`COMMIT` of their own.
2. **Discord:** register the new slash commands once with `npm run register -w bot`. A deploy does not register commands in this release.
