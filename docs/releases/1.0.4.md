# Release 1.0.4

1.0.4 is the privacy release. Members can download everything the club holds about them, account deletion erases more of what it should, and execs can mint keys for the Data API. It also carries a round of season fixes, so that past seasons stop borrowing today's numbers. It reached production on 2026-09-22.

Compared with 1.0.3. The leaderboard and season fixes below first reached production on 2026-09-20, in the build still numbered 1.0.3; this is the release that records them.

## For members

### Your data

- Settings, Privacy, "Download my data": one JSON file of what the club holds about you. Anything it leaves out is listed in the file with the reason, rather than silently missing.
- Deleting your account now also removes your Discord link (and with it your club roles in Discord), detaches your feedback reports from you, and removes your calendar feed link.

### Seasons and the ladder

- The leaderboard marks your own row, and its column key matches the columns actually drawn.
- Tournaments has a season picker; a past season shows that season's tournaments and nothing else.
- A public profile counts its record from the season's own matches, and is not read at all for a member who has opted out of the leaderboard.
- The Feed's match results are scoped to the current season.
- The Discord card's NIGHTS panel counts this season's nights, not every night ever.
- The weekly recap leaves out Elo figures for a week in which a season rolled over, since they would mix two ladders.

## For execs and admins

- Data API keys: mint and revoke them from Accounts. Keys are stored hashed. (The service that answers them came in 1.1.0.)
- Eight permission baselines named after the club's real officer roles, and a level now comes with a baseline. Every officer can file an expense.
- The permission editor shows what an officer already holds through the level floor.
- `/forceunlink` disconnects somebody else's Discord account, and `/forceupdate` resyncs one member's roles. Console messages that pointed at a non-existent unlink control now name `/forceunlink`.
- Admin Challenges counts the whole club rather than the newest 50 rows, and the dashboard counts who is on the ladder rather than who has a rating row.
- A member's page, on a past season, shows that season's record rather than today's numbers under the season's name.
- An officer who has only a member-site passkey can now enrol a console passkey from Settings.
- The console's toggle switch is square, like every other control there.

### Other

- The deletion purges share one list of what to erase, and a test fails when a new table that points at a member has not been classified.
- The repo's copies of the legal documents now name the club, the app and the privacy contact, settle eligibility at 19 or older (or with a guardian's consent), and state the effective date as the version members accepted.

## Before you deploy / owner steps

1. **Apply the migrations in numeric order**, 00236, 00237, 00238, 00241. 00236, 00237 and 00238 have no `BEGIN`/`COMMIT` of their own.
2. **Edge Functions:** deploy the updated account purge functions.
3. **Discord:** register `/forceunlink` and `/forceupdate` (automatic if command registration on boot is switched on, otherwise `npm run register -w bot`).
4. **Legal documents:** members see the revised text only once it is published from the console.
