# Release 1.0.6

1.0.6 is the My stats hotfix: every card on My stats now follows the season you pick. It also carries the iPhone passkey fix and faster signed-in pages. It reached production on 2026-09-29.

Compared with 1.0.5. The iPhone passkey fix first reached production on 2026-09-27, and the faster pages on 2026-09-28, in builds still numbered 1.0.5; this is the release that records them.

## For members

- My stats: the match table, recent form, rating line, head-to-head and partners all show only the chosen season. Before, they mixed in matches from earlier seasons.
- The season picker offers the current season and every finished season you played in, and always names the season the page covers. Hidden seasons never appear.
- Every My stats section names the season in its empty state.
- On iPhone, one tap on the passkey button opens the passkey sheet. Before, a slow connection could make the tap do nothing.
- Signed-in pages, the Feed and Settings wait on fewer round trips before they show.

## Under the hood

- Passkey options are fetched before the tap, so the ceremony starts directly from it, as iOS Safari requires. This covers member sign-in, member passkey enrolment and console sign-in.
- The member site's middleware passes its verified sign-in answer to the page render on a short-lived signed header, so a page no longer asks the auth server twice. A missing or stale value falls back to the full check.

### Measured speed

Medians from a signed-in browser on the staging copy of the site, with the first rounds discarded as warm-up.

| What | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asking the auth server who is signed in | 52 to 120 ms | 2 to 6 ms |
| A signed-in page, start to finish | 250 to 510 ms | 120 to 200 ms |
| Auth server checks for 10 signed-in page loads | 20 | 10 |
| Browser round trips, one after another, before Settings fills in | 4 | 1 (3 reads at once) |

- The first two rows are mostly the auth server keeping a few database connections open between requests. Before, every check opened about five new ones, and each opening cost far more than the queries themselves, which take under 1 ms. That was a server setting changed on 2026-09-28, not code in this release.
- The production auth server's own logs before that change showed a median of 90 ms to check a sign-in, and 226 ms to refresh one (1 second at the 95th percentile).
- The third row is this release's own change: the page reuses the answer the middleware already verified, on a signed header valid for 30 seconds. A missing or stale answer falls back to the full check, so the worst case is the old speed.
- The Feed now loads you, the season and the tournament reads together instead of one after another.
- Not changed: refreshing a sign-in still takes about 230 ms, but happens about once an hour per member. Shorter sign-in lifetimes keep sign-out and bans quick, so it stays.
