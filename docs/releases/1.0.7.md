# Release 1.0.7

1.0.7 brings two rating rules, unrated events for external teams, and tournament desk fixes. It reached production on 2026-09-29.

Compared with 1.0.6. Everything below except the check-in undo and the round robin button label first reached production about an hour earlier the same day, in a build still numbered 1.0.6.

## For members

- Repeat challenges count for less: each earlier confirmed rated challenge against the same opponent (or the same doubles teams) within 30 days cuts the rating change by 25 percent, never below 10 percent of the full change (the defaults, which execs can tune). Past ratings are not recalculated.
- A tournament's start date shows the right day; before, it could show the day before.
- Doubles round robins and final standings head their column Pair, not Player.
- An event for external teams says it is entered by the organisers.
- Confirming a rated challenge result no longer fails at the rating step (00268).

## For execs and admins

- External events: mark an event as external, add teams by name with no member account, and play an unrated round robin. Results move nobody's rating, and standings rank by wins, then point difference, then points for.
- Edit the repeat-challenge window, cut and floor from a card on Matches (admins also see them on Ratings).
- Boost one confirmed, rated match in the active season (not a walkover or a tournament match) by up to 2x. The boost is audited, and voiding the match undoes it.
- Check-in desk: Undo a check-in or a no-show while check-in is open. A no-show comes back as waiting, so the waiver check still runs at the door.
- A plain round robin's button says "Generate Round Robin", not "Generate Bracket".
