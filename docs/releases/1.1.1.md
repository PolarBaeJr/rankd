# Release 1.1.1

1.1.1 brings console work to Discord. Execs can create, edit, archive and delete sessions, run club events and run a tournament on the day with `/session`, `/event` and `/tourney`, as themselves and under their own console permissions. Members get `/schedule` for their next two weeks, a full-page calendar at `/calendar`, tournaments in their calendar feed, and `/tournaments` to enter, read draws, find their next match and follow results. It also carries the passkey fix for in-app browsers. Google Forms now feed the fee review: an event form's e-transfer screenshots reach Submitted receipts, a form can pay a season's dues, and a draft event takes its form so the mapping can be tested before the event is announced. The console's top bar becomes a menu on phones, and the site shows the club's own page when it cannot reach the app.

Compared with 1.1.0.

## For members

### Schedule and calendar

- `/schedule` in Discord shows your own next two weeks: the sessions on your track, published club events and tournaments, by day in club time. Only you see the reply. It ends with a link to add the club calendar to your phone, and a link to the calendar page.
- `/schedule` asks you to run `/link` first if your Discord account is not linked to the club.
- A new Calendar page (`/calendar`) shows the month calendar on its own, on a phone as well as a computer. The Feed's "Up next" has a Full calendar button that opens it.
- On the Calendar page, an upcoming open session links to its card on the Feed.
- Tournaments now appear in the subscribed calendar feed as all-day events, on every day they run.

### Tournaments on Discord

- `/tournaments list` (or `/tournaments` on its own) marks the tournaments happening now and puts them first, and lists the ones that finished in the last week.
- `/tournaments enter` enters you in a singles event as yourself. The event picker lists only the events taking entries now, and every rule the website applies (the entry window, capacity, membership type, category and the entry cap) applies here too, in the website's own words.
- A tournament with its own event waiver, and every doubles event, is entered on the website. `/tournaments enter` says so and links to the event page, because accepting a waiver or a partner arrangement is the website's to ask.
- `/tournaments draw` shows an event's draw: each match side A first with its score, by round, and pool standings for a round robin. A big draw is split across several embeds, with a Full draw link to the website.
- `/tournaments next` shows your own next match: the round, who you play, the court and whether it is on court or waiting to be called.
- `/tournaments results` shows what is on court in a tournament and its last ten results, including a tournament that has just finished.
- Every reply is shown only to you. Draft tournaments never appear, and nothing appears while tournaments are switched off for the club.
- If the club app does not answer an entry in time, `/tournaments enter` says it cannot tell whether you were entered and to check the website, rather than claiming nothing happened.

### Sign-in

- Passkey sign-in in an app's built-in browser (for example Instagram or Facebook) no longer fails silently. When the passkey prompt is blocked, the page says to open it in Safari or Chrome, or to use an email code. This covers member sign-in and enrolment, console sign-in and adding a console passkey.

## For execs and admins

### Google Forms and fees

- A Google Form's e-transfer screenshot (a file upload question) reaches Submitted receipts on /fees and the tournament fees page, on the fee it pays. OCR reads the reference, and Confirm marks the fee paid as for a member's own receipt.
- **Erase screenshot** deletes a kept form screenshot after confirming (a privacy request); the fee stays paid and the receipt row stays.
- **A form can pay a season's dues.** Link it on /fees, under Submitted, to the season shown. Each response's screenshot is filed on that person's dues: a member's own row at their tier (pending approval included), anyone without an account a named row at the recreational price, which follows them when they sign up with that email. Execs, fee-exempt, banned and leaving members are not charged.
- **A draft club event takes its form.** Responses are imported while the event is a draft, so the question mapping can be tested before it is announced. A non-member gets their place and fee; a member's "Confirm your sign-up" notification is held until the event is published, and sent once.
- Named dues rows on /fees now show Paid, Waived or Unpaid; they always said Paid before. An unpaid one has **Mark Paid**, for money that came some other way than its screenshot.
- The form card says when the service account needs an Editor share, and only suggests asking for the screenshot again when a resend would help.

### Console on phones

- Below laptop width the console's top bar shows the mark and a menu button. The menu lists every section under its group, with your email, Member app and Sign out at the bottom. The desktop bar is unchanged.

### When the site is down

- A worker at the edge shows the club's own "Back soon." page, with Try again and the club Discord, instead of the bare gateway error pages, on page loads only. API calls, server actions and maintenance mode pass through untouched, and the real status is kept. Its source and setup are kept out of the public export.


### Console commands on Discord

- `/session create`: a session with date, start, end, location and track, and optionally a weekly series up to a date. Name, notes and the series are optional.
- `/session edit`: change any field of an open session. A reason is required, as in the console.
- `/session archive` and `/session delete` open a form that asks for the reason. Delete says that its RSVPs and attendance go with it.
- `/session list`, `/session attendance` (who is going and who checked in) and `/session checkin` (the check-in link, or a fresh one with `rotate`), all shown only to you.
- `/event create` (a draft unless `publish` is set), `/event publish`, `/event cancel` (asks for a reason), `/event delete` (type DELETE to confirm), `/event list` and `/event signups`.
- An event that people have signed up for cannot be deleted; `/event delete` says to use `/event cancel` instead.
- Session and event options autocomplete from the console.
- Every command runs the console's own action as the exec whose Discord account is linked. The same capability checks apply, and a refusal shows the console's own words and code. Nothing is possible on Discord that the exec cannot do in the console.
- Changes made from Discord appear in the audit log as the exec, marked `source: discord`.
- **Passkey policy:** Discord console commands need a console passkey on the exec's account, or the exec must still be inside the console passkey grace window. Otherwise the bot says: "Add a console passkey in the console's Settings first. Discord console commands need one on your account." The policy lives in one function, `discordPasskeyPolicy` in `apps/admin/src/lib/discord-actor.ts`.
- Both commands are hidden from members by default. A server admin grants them to the exec role (see owner steps).

### Running a tournament from Discord

- `/tourney checkin`, `/tourney noshow` and `/tourney undo-checkin` act on a player or pair picked from the tournament's entries.
- `/tourney result` takes the games as `21-15 19-21 21-17`, side A first, as shown in the match picker. The winner is worked out from the games by the console's own rule, and the reply names both sides, for example "Recorded: Smith 21-15 Jones". A score where both sides won the same number of games is refused.
- `/tourney walkover` (side A or B, with a reason), `/tourney court` and `/tourney live` (start or stop).
- `/tourney status` sets the tournament's status. `/tourney suspend` opens a form for the reason members see; `/tourney resume` lifts it.
- `/tourney fee` marks a player's tournament fee paid (with an optional method) or unpaid.
- `/tourney checkin-link` gets the self check-in link, or issues a new one with `rotate`. The link is a credential: it is shown only to you and never posted.
- Every picker starts from the tournament you picked, so a match or entry from another tournament is never offered.
- Same gates as `/session` and `/event`: the console's own action as the linked exec, the passkey policy, `source: discord` in the audit log. Hidden from members by default.

## Under the hood

### Security and auth

- New console routes `/api/discord/actions/<name>` and `/api/discord/reads/<name>`, gated by the shared Discord service secret. Each runs a fixed list of console actions and reads. Arguments are checked strictly, so a misspelt field is refused rather than read as "clear it".
- The caller is resolved from the Discord link on every request. The console's own gate then loads that exec's row instead of reading a cookie. It applies the same standing checks (banned, suspended, pending, inactive) and the same capability check as the console.
- The cookie step-up does not run on this path. The passkey policy above replaces it, and a failed read refuses (AUTH-103) rather than passes.
- Nine new capability gate entries for the Discord reads, all on existing capabilities (`sessions.page`, `events.page`, `events.signups.read`, `tournaments.page`, `tournaments.fees.read`). No new capabilities.
- `/tournaments enter` goes through the same play gate as a Discord challenge (link, standing, the tournaments switch, the club's legal documents), then the website's own entry code. It never records an event waiver acceptance.
- An error the console has not coded is never shown on Discord, because it may be raw database text. The bot says something went wrong instead.

### APIs and services

- Player app: `GET /api/discord/schedule` builds a member's two weeks with the same track filter as the Feed, skips switched-off features, and is a 503 when a read fails rather than an empty schedule. The calendar feed link is created only for a member in good standing.
- The calendar feed token is created in one shared place (`lib/calendar-feed-token.ts`), used by both the Settings action and the Discord route.
- The bot calls the console with its own base URL, `ADMIN_API_URL`, and does not follow redirects. An older console without these routes answers "This command is not available yet." rather than a confusing error.
- Player app: `GET /api/discord/tournament-picker`, `/tournament-draw`, `/tournament-next`, `/tournament-results` and `POST /api/discord/tournament-entry`. The reads name their columns and never read `notes`.
- The web's tournament entry body moved into `lib/tournament-entry-core.ts` so the website and Discord share it. The website's behaviour is unchanged.
- `GET /api/discord/tournaments` adds `inProgress` to each tournament and a `recentlyFinished` list (omitted on a search, and empty if its read fails).
- Console reads `tournaments`, `tournament-entries`, `tournament-matches` and `tournament-fees`, and the tournament actions behind `/tourney`.

### Database migrations

- `00288_form_receipts_reach_the_review`: form uploads become receipts in fee_submissions (record, claim, settle, file), the Erase screenshot action, and receipts that follow a named fee to a new account.
- `00289_a_draft_event_takes_its_form`: a draft club event takes form responses; a member's notification waits for publication.
- `00290_a_season_takes_its_form`: a form linked to a season files its receipts on that season's dues. Needs 00289 first.

## Before you deploy / owner steps

0. **Migrations:** apply 00288, 00289 and 00290 to prod in that order, before the deploy. Each is verified by its own check block.
1. **Bot environment:** set `ADMIN_API_URL` on the bot to the console's base URL, including its path prefix, in the same place `APP_API_URL` is set for the bot. Without it, `/session` and `/event` reply that console commands are not configured.
2. **Console environment:** set `DISCORD_SERVICE_SECRET` on the console through the dashboard with `ref:NAME`, the same secret the bot and the member app use. The console now checks it on its own `/api/discord/` routes, and refuses every call while it is unset.
3. **Register the commands:** run `npm run register -w bot` once after the bot deploys, so `/schedule`, `/session`, `/event`, `/tourney` and the new `/tournaments` subcommands appear. A deploy does not register commands. If it was already run for an earlier 1.1.1 build, re-run it.
4. **Grant the exec commands:** in Discord, Server Settings, Integrations, the bot, give the exec role access to `/session`, `/event` and `/tourney`. They are hidden from everyone else by default.
5. **Edge rate limit:** consider a limit on the console's `/api/discord/` path, like the member app's.
6. **Passkey policy:** the default is described under "For execs and admins". Changing it is a change to `discordPasskeyPolicy` alone.

## Known limitations

- Discord commands cannot do the cookie step-up, so a recent passkey check is not asked for; an enrolled console passkey is enough.
- `/session edit` changes one session, not a whole series.
- The calendar feed shows tournaments by their dates only, with no venue.
- The Calendar page has no nav entry; it is reached from the Feed.
- `/tournaments enter` is singles only, and only for tournaments without their own event waiver. Withdrawing is on the website.
- `/tournaments draw` is capped at 400 lines; a bigger draw ends with a link to the website.
- A form's screenshots reach the review only for responses imported after 00288 is live; earlier ones stay in the form's Drive folder.
- The service account must be an Editor on each form; a Viewer share reads the form but not its responses.
