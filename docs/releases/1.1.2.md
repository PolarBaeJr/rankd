# Release 1.1.2

1.1.2 lets a club event run as a tournament. An event that needs a bracket, scores or check-in gets a linked tournament event, and its sign-ups and the tournament's entries stay the same both ways. Non-members who signed up on the event's Google Form are entered as guests: no login, no dues, sent nothing. A member who answers an event's form is now signed up straight away. On Discord, `/softban` removes a spammer and their recent messages, and a server log records what happens in the server.

Compared with 1.1.1.

## For members

- Answering a club event's Google Form signs you up at once; there is no "Confirm your sign-up" step for a club event any more. You get an in-app notice (no email), sent when the event is published.
- If the form signed you up by mistake, **Not me** on the event takes the sign-up back, with its unpaid fee, and tells the exec who linked the form.
- If you signed the waiver on the event's Google Form, the app does not ask you to sign it again for the linked tournament, and you can check yourself in.
- Guests do not appear on the leaderboard, in the challenge opponent picker or on public profiles, and cannot be challenged.

## For execs and admins

### A club event run as a tournament

- A club event has a **Tournament** card: pick the event type and format, choose whether results count toward ratings, and **Run as a tournament**. A draft tournament event is made, and everyone signed up is entered.
- From then on a sign-up on the club event is an entry in the tournament and the other way round. The fee is charged once, on the club event.
- Once the draw is out or locked, adding or removing a sign-up is refused with a reason, so the bracket is not changed behind its safeguards.
- Everyone the Google Form entered counts as having signed the waiver: check-in, the draw gate and the roster's waiver column treat them as signed.
- Tournaments keep their confirm step for form sign-ups; only club events sign up at once.

### Guests

- A non-member from the Form is entered as a guest: recreational, external, no login. Guests are sent no notifications or email, are kept off dues lists and member counts, and their waiver invite is cancelled because the Form carried the waiver.
- Players has a **Guests** view (hidden while there are none), and the views are now one View menu. Guests carry a Guest label.
- A guest who signs in with the same email becomes a normal member, keeping their record.
- Making a guest a member without a sign-in is an admin's decision: an exec asks, and an admin confirms or declines with a reason. Execs cannot change a guest's status or membership.
- **Erase** removes a guest's personal data and keeps the record as "Former guest". An exec can export a guest's data on request.
- No Discord account can be linked to a guest.
- **Add Player** now makes a guest unless a division is picked. Adding a player with Competitive or Recreational picked works again.

### Discord moderation

- `/softban member [delete] [reason]` bans someone and deletes their messages from the last hour, day (the default) or 7 days, then unbans them at once so they can rejoin with the invite. It needs Discord's Ban Members permission, and only works on someone ranked below you. If the unban fails, the reply and the audit log say they are still banned. It does not touch their club account.
- The **server log**, set with `/config channels server_log`, records bans, kicks, timeouts, role and channel changes, joins (with the account's age), leaves, and deleted and edited messages, with their text. Make it an exec-only channel.

## Under the hood

### Database migrations

- **00291** `a_form_signs_a_member_up`: a club event form's member sign-up and its "Not me" undo.
- **00292** `a_club_event_runs_as_a_tournament`: the linked tournament, the two-way sync, guests, their erase and membership flow, and the Add Player fix.

### Offline

- When the network drops and a page is not cached, the member app's service worker now returns an ordinary network error instead of failing itself, which Safari reported as an error.

### Export

- The public export no longer fails on the admin app's vendored OCR files.

## Before you deploy / owner steps

0. **Migrations:** apply 00291 then 00292 to prod, before the deploy. Each is verified by its own check block.
1. **Discord developer portal** (the production app, Bot page): turn on **Server Members Intent** and **Message Content Intent**.
2. **Discord server:** give the bot's role **Ban Members** and **View Audit Log**, above the member roles. Make an exec-only log channel the bot can post in.
3. **Bot environment:** set `DISCORD_SERVER_LOG=moderation,members,messages,content`, in the same place as the bot's other settings. Unset, the server log is off.
4. **Register the commands:** run `npm run register -w bot` once after the bot deploys, so `/softban` and the new `/config` option appear.
5. **Point the log:** `/config channels server_log:` and pick the log channel.
6. **Regenerate the database types** from prod once 00291 and 00292 are applied.

## Known limitations

- The server log keeps the text of messages sent while the bot is running; a message older than the running bot shows as not cached when deleted.
- If a privileged intent is not turned on in the portal, the bot stays online and the server log stays quiet, with a line in the bot's log.
- A guest made from the Form is recreational; change their division after they become a member.
