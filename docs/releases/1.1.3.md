# Release 1.1.3

1.1.3 is about Google Forms and receipts. The console can make an event's Google Form itself and keep it in step with the event. The waiver answers on a Form sign the guest waiver. A receipt the server reads cleanly confirms itself, with nobody looking.

Compared with 1.1.2.

## For members

- A receipt whose screenshot the server can read (the payee, the amount and a reference that match the fee) is confirmed on its own, usually within a minute. Anything it cannot read waits for an exec as before.

## For execs and admins

### Google Forms

- An event or tournament can have its Google Form made by the console: the questions, the waiver, the receipt upload and the event choices, kept in step as the event changes. Until the Form maker is configured, this stays off.
- A Form's waiver answer and signature sign the guest waiver for whoever answered, so the app does not ask them again.

### Club events

- A club event's row opens the event. Places taken count Form guests too.
- The event page has one **Signed up** list for members and Form guests, a cancel dialog that asks for a reason, and a date and time picker in the console's theme.
- **Print** gives a sheet of an event's groups and match schedule.
- The events list hides **Past** until an event has happened.

### Fees

- A receipt's actions sit on one line.
- Guests leave **New this week**, and the roster's view is a menu of ticks.

## Under the hood

### Database migrations

- **00295** `a_form_signs_the_guest_waiver`: a Form's waiver answers sign the guest waiver.
- **00296** `the_console_makes_the_forms`: the Forms the console makes and keeps in step.
- **00297** `a_read_receipt_confirms_itself`: the server's read of a receipt, and confirming one with nobody looking.

### Dependencies

- Next 15.5.28, sharp 0.35.5 and Node 24.22.1, for the October advisories.

## Before you deploy / owner steps

0. **Migrations:** apply 00295, 00296 then 00297 to prod, before the deploy.
1. **Settings:** set the **E-transfer payee name** in the console's Settings, or no receipt can confirm itself.
