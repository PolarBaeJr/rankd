# Release 1.1.4

1.1.4 makes reviewing a receipt one step, and lets a Form receipt with no reference be approved.

Compared with 1.1.3.

## For execs and admins

- Each receipt has one **Review** button that opens the screenshot with **Approve** and **Reject** beside it. **Erase** stays on the row.
- A receipt uploaded on a Google Form carries no typed reference. It can now be approved by an exec, or confirmed by the reader when the payee and amount match; the screenshot's hash stops the same picture paying twice.
- A receipt sent before its event, tournament or season was made, or after it ended, is held for an exec instead of confirming itself.

## Under the hood

### Database migrations

- **00298** `a_form_receipt_confirms_without_a_reference`: approving a reference-less Form receipt, the screenshot hash, and the hold for a receipt outside its window.

### Export

- The receipt test fixtures name no club, and a release train dry-runs the public export.
