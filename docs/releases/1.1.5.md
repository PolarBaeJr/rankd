# Release 1.1.5

1.1.5 teaches the receipt reader more banks' screenshots, and fixes a Form mapping saved before the waiver questions existed.

Compared with 1.1.4.

## For execs and admins

- The receipt reader finds an amount printed large on its own (it re-reads a smaller copy when the first read finds none), reads a bank's "transfer deposited" notice as an e-transfer, and the **E-transfer payee name** setting takes several names, separated by commas.
- A Form's question mapping saved before the waiver questions existed now starts them from their defaults instead of blank.

## Under the hood

- A release branch is checked by CI when pushed, and the production build of that same commit skips the repeat.

## Before you deploy / owner steps

No migration.

1. **Settings:** list every name e-transfers arrive under in **E-transfer payee name**, separated by commas.
2. **Forms:** open the mapping of any Form saved before 1.1.3 and save it, so its waiver answers are read.
