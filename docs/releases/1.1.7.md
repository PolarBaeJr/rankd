# Release 1.1.7

1.1.7 records what a receipt actually paid, shows a Form sign-up's preferred partner, and shows saved settings as text with an Edit button.

Compared with 1.1.6.

## For execs and admins

- **Approve** on a receipt asks for the **Amount paid**, filled in from what the reader saw. The **Auto-confirmed** list is gone from Fees; **Mark unpaid** undoes a confirmation the reader got wrong.
- A Form's "preferred partner" answer shows beside the person on the event's **Signed up** list.
- Saved records (settings and the like) read as text, with an **Edit** button to change them.
- The Fees paste box's example reads name, email, amount, and says the email is optional.

## Under the hood

### Database migrations

- **00299** `a_signup_remembers_its_preferred_partner`: `preferred_partner` on both club event sign-up tables, and the Form mapping's partner question.
- **00300** `a_reviewed_receipt_records_the_amount_paid`: the amount an exec approves a receipt at.
