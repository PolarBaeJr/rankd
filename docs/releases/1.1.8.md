# Release 1.1.8

1.1.8 gathers everything since 1.1.7; fixes were added to it instead of bumping the version each time. For members: a paid club event takes the e-transfer screenshot with the sign-up. For execs: an officer can add somebody to a club event by hand, the event page updates live, Fees is one list with the club's money on its own Finances page, and every exec can read everything outside the admin areas. On Discord the bot works in any server, with auto mod, a trap channel, `/purge`, `/event signups` and a server log that names who did what.

Compared with 1.1.7.

## For members

- **A paid club event asks for the e-transfer screenshot when you sign up.** **Sign up** opens a pop-up with the amount and where to send it; attach the screenshot and the reference, and you are signed up and your receipt is filed together. If the receipt cannot be filed, you are not signed up. **Take the place** from the waitlist works the same way. Execs, fee-exempt members and free events sign up in one tap as before, and so does anyone whose fee for the event is already paid or has a receipt.
- A tournament shows its own start and end times and its own location, in the app and on Discord's Events tab.
- `/discord` works for every member, so anyone can share the server invite.

## For execs and admins

### Club events

- **Add person** on the event's **Signed up** card adds somebody by hand: a member, or someone with no account at all, by name only. Email, photo consent, a preferred partner and the Form's other questions are all optional, required ones included, and no waiver is asked for. If the event costs money, **Payment** records how they paid (cash by default) or leaves the fee unpaid. Everyone added this way is a player record, so if the event later runs as a tournament they are entered like anybody else. Someone with no email is never sent mail.
- The event and check-in pages update live when someone signs up, a Form is imported, the waitlist moves or someone is checked in.
- The **Signed up** list shows newest first, and a member's row shows their fee as paid or unpaid, as a guest's does.
- **Cancel** sits in the page header; **Run as a tournament** and the Google Form link open from buttons.

### Fees and Finances

- Fees is one member list with a **View** menu. The club's own money (other income and expenses) has moved to a new **Finances** page; old links redirect.
- A deleted member is no longer on the dues list.
- The receipt reader reads RBC's screenshots, whose amount sits under an "Amount Date" line.

### Permissions

- Every exec can read everything outside the five admin areas (permissions, audit, ratings, accounts and platform settings). Writes still come from roles.
- The permission editor offers every write outside the admin areas, including the club events writes that could not be ticked before. Existing roles were given the writes that follow from ones they already held.
- Permissions, courts and Discord linking show as text first, with an Edit button.

### Tournaments

- A tournament has its own start and end times and location, set on the console or with `/tourney`, instead of one club-wide time and place.

## On Discord

- **Any server.** The bot works in any server it is added to. `/setup` sets one up, and does more in a club server; only a club officer can change the club's own setup. Each server keeps its own settings under `/config`, including its log channel.
- **Auto mod**, tuned per server under `/config`: soft-bans an account that sprays the same post across channels, among other rules.
- **Trap channel**: posting in it soft-bans the account. The channel pins one warning card, with a stats button counting who it caught.
- **`/purge`** deletes the latest messages in a channel, or one person's. With `include_old` it also deletes messages older than 14 days, one at a time.
- **`/event signups`** lists an event's sign-ups to those allowed to see them, each marked **form**, **app** or **manual** by how they got on the list.
- **The server log** names who did each thing, and who deleted a message when a moderator did; a message somebody deleted themselves shows without a name. A burst of role changes posts as one entry, grouped by role. A soft ban logs as a soft ban by its moderator, and a member leaving no longer guesses at kicks and bans.
- A console picker says why it is empty when the console refuses.

## Under the hood

### Database migrations

- **00301** `an_event_page_hears_its_signups_change`: the `club_event_activity` signal table the event pages subscribe to.
- **00302** `each_discord_server_keeps_its_own_settings`: a settings row per Discord server.
- **00303** `each_server_tunes_its_own_auto_mod`: each server's auto mod rules.
- **00304** `a_tournament_keeps_its_own_times_and_place`: a tournament's own times and location.
- **00305** `roles_take_the_writes_nobody_could_tick`: fills existing roles with the writes the editor could not offer.
- **00306** `an_officer_adds_somebody_by_hand`: `club_event_add_by_hand`, and `answers` on club event sign-ups.

### Other

- Errors from scripts an in-app browser injects are dropped before reaching Sentry.
- The mailer refuses any `.invalid` address, so a guest added with no email can never cause a bounce.

## Before you deploy / owner steps

0. **Migrations:** apply 00301 to 00306 to prod, in order, before the deploy.
1. **Register the commands:** run `npm run register -w bot` once the bot deploys, so `/purge`, `/event signups`, `/tourney` and the new `/config` options appear.
2. **Regenerate the database types** from prod once the migrations are applied.

## Known limitations

- An app sign-up's screenshot is checked by the reader or an exec afterwards; being signed up does not mean the payment is confirmed.
- A Form sign-up's screenshot is required by the Google Form itself. A Form made by hand without that question marked required would let a response through without one.
